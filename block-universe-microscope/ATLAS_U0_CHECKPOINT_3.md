## Boundary-at-infinity audit

U₀ is non-proper: sequences with `||x||→∞` can converge to finite output states because tanh saturates.

The infinity structure is not featureless. Generic escape directions fall into 4096 sign sectors. Each sector approaches a distinct asymptotic state:

- minimum distinct sector-state separation in the current output chart ≈ 0.09950
- no duplicate generic sector state was found

The 12192 boundary edge-curves were sampled and their closest unrelated pair was numerically minimized; the best residual remained ≈ 0.07856, with no evidence of an exact edge self-identification.

Each of the 4048 boundary facets can be parameterized by the three first-layer units that remain unsaturated while the other 21 units saturate to ±1. Across sampled facet interiors:

- every sampled facet derivative had rank 3,
- weakest sampled facet singular value ≈ 0.0222,
- no interior facet-facet self-intersection was found; closest approaches occurred as optimization moved toward source-face boundaries, consistent with ordinary facet adjacency.

Current boundary hypothesis: the compactified boundary map is an embedded regular 3D boundary complex, though this is not yet a proof.

## Defect stability under nearby-universe perturbation

A one-parameter family of nearby frozen networks was generated by adding a fixed small random perturbation direction to all weights/biases. This does **not** modify U₀; it probes structural stability in neighboring universes.

The six transverse defects persist initially as moving exact roots, as expected for transverse intersections.

One positive defect terminates at a finite critical event near

- `epsilon_c ≈ 0.04290674`
- source point ≈ `(-0.563375, -0.784943, -3.116548, -3.053611)`

At that critical point the weakest source Jacobian singular value drops to approximately `6.4e-14`, i.e. the nearby universe itself leaves the immersion class.

Approaching the event from below, the two preimages coalesce with

`separation² ∝ (epsilon_c - epsilon)`

with fit `R² ≈ 0.999993`, giving clean square-root / fold-like scaling.

This matches the codimension count: the rank-≤3 locus in the space of 8×4 matrices has codimension `(8-3)(4-3)=5`. A single frozen 4D source generically misses it, while a one-parameter family has 4+1=5 dimensions and can hit isolated rank-drop events.

Interpretation: transverse double-fiber defects are stable inside the immersion regime, but an arbitrary family of universes can create/destroy them by passing through isolated singular maps. If one constrains evolution to remain within immersions, opposite-sign double points are instead the natural objects canceled by Whitney moves in dimension 4→8.

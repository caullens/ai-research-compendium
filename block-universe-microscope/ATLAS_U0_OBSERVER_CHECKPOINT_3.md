# Atlas U₀ — Observer Ontology Checkpoint 3: nonlinear sensors

This checkpoint tests whether the observer-relative singularity structure found under linear projections survives genuinely nonlinear sensory maps.

## Nonlinear 7-channel observers

Three independent sensory maps of the form

`H(s) = A s + alpha C tanh(B s + c)`

were sampled, each mapping the full 8D state into 7 observed channels.

For each observer:

1. The exact full-state defect D0 was re-examined through `H o F`.
2. The derivative of the pair-equality condition at D0 had rank 7 in the 8D source-pair space, leaving a one-dimensional projected equality locus.
3. A bounded root search recovered multiple isolated projected critical points where `rank D(H o F) = 3` while the full U₀ Jacobian remained rank 4.
4. Every tested critical point passed the generalized 4->7 cross-cap second-order nondegeneracy test.

Results:

- nonlinear observer 1: D0 equality-locus dimension 1; 6 isolated critical points found; 6/6 tested cross-caps
- nonlinear observer 2: dimension 1; 7 critical points; 7/7 cross-caps
- nonlinear observer 3: dimension 1; 11 critical points; first 8/8 tested cross-caps

Thus the `k=7` observer pattern is not an artifact of linear projection:

- one-dimensional distant self-identification loci,
- isolated observer-induced critical points,
- generalized cross-cap local singularity type

persist under generic smooth nonlinear sensory maps.

## Interpretation

The observer laws are best understood as transversality/dimension phenomena of composing a 4D immersed world with a 7D observation map, not as special properties of orthogonal projection.

This substantially strengthens the claim that observer-effective ontology depends on sensory dimension and generic information loss, rather than on the particular linear camera used in the first experiments.
